BACKGROUND MUSIC
================
bgm.mp3 in this folder is the invitation's music. It starts as soon as a guest taps "Open the doors",
begins from 0:24 of the song, and fades in very softly (about 6 seconds). When the song ends it eases out
and comes back in softly from 0:24 again. A small round button at the bottom left lets guests pause or play.

To change it (all in ../config.js):
  music: 'music/bgm.mp3'   the file name (keep the file in this folder)
  musicStart: '24'         start point in seconds
  musicVolume: '0.5'       how loud it gets, from 0.1 (very soft) to 1

To use another song, replace bgm.mp3 with your own file of the same name. Keep it under about 6 MB
(a 3 to 4 minute mp3 at 128 kbps is roughly 3 MB).

Only use a track you have the right to share publicly. Commercial film songs are copyrighted and the page
could be taken down; a licensed or royalty-free track avoids that.
